If you're worried about your partner

Postpartum psychosis is rare (about 1 to 2 in 1,000 births). It usually starts in the first few weeks after birth but can come later, and it is a medical emergency. Call 911 if she:

  • Talks about harming herself or the baby
  • Seems confused or out of touch with reality
  • Sees or hears things that aren't there
  • Hasn't slept in days or seems unusually wired
  • Has strange beliefs or is suddenly very suspicious or paranoid
  • Has rapid, extreme mood swings

Many new moms with postpartum depression or anxiety get sudden, unwanted thoughts or images of something bad happening to the baby, and are horrified by them. These thoughts are common and treatable, and having them doesn't mean she'll act on them. Help her call her doctor or the Maternal Mental Health Hotline the same day. Call 911 if she seems to want to act on them or seems out of touch with reality.

For anything less urgent but still worrying, call or text the Maternal Mental Health Hotline at 1-833-852-6262. Partners can call on her behalf.
