If you're worried about your partner

Postpartum psychosis is rare, but it is a medical emergency. Call 911 if she:

  • Talks about harming herself or the baby
  • Seems confused or out of touch with reality
  • Sees or hears things that aren't there
  • Hasn't slept in days or seems unusually wired

For anything less urgent but still worrying, call the Maternal Mental Health Hotline above. Partners can call on her behalf.
