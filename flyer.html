Postpartum Fathers

Free support for new and expecting dads

Dads go through postpartum too.

Short, plain-language guides for fathers, written for the dad who is worn out at 2am, plus how to help a partner who is struggling. Free, anonymous, and in English and Spanish. No ads, no sign-up, nothing for sale.

Does any of this sound like you?

  • “I’m angry all the time.”
  • “I feel numb.”
  • “I can’t sleep, even when the baby sleeps.”
  • “I’m worried about her.”

These are common in the first year, and they get better with support.

Thinking about suicide or in crisis? Call or text 988 any time. Emergency: 911.

Postpartum Support International HelpLine (support and referrals, dads too, not a crisis line): call 1-800-944-4773 or text “Help” to 800-944-4773.

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline (24/7, call or text, dads too): 1-833-852-6262

Postpartum Fathers is peer support, not medical advice, and doesn’t replace care from a doctor or licensed mental health professional. Clinics are welcome to copy and post this flyer. Spanish version: postpartumfathers.org/es/flyer.html
