Dads go through postpartum too.

About 1 in 10 new fathers become depressed after a baby arrives, and dads whose partners have postpartum depression are at even higher risk. It’s common, it’s treatable, and you don’t have to figure it out alone.

  • Short temper or anger
  • Drinking or escaping more
  • Pulling away from family
  • Feeling numb or hopeless
  • Working late to avoid home
  • Worried about your partner
