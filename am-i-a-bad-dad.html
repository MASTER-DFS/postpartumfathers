You’re doing better than you think

Feel like you’re failing as a dad? Read this

If you're lying awake asking whether you're a bad dad, here's something worth knowing: dads who don't care don't ask that question. The fact that you're worried says a lot about how much this matters to you. Feeling like you're failing is common, and it usually says more about how tired you are than how good a father you are.

Rough night? Call or text 988, or 1-833-852-6262 (24/7, dads too).

Why the feeling shows up

  • Nobody hands you a manual. Most of parenting is figuring it out as you go.
  • Comparing yourself to dads online, or to the father you thought you'd be.
  • The baby only wants Mom sometimes, especially early on. It's normal and it changes.
  • You lost your temper, missed something, or got it wrong. Every parent does.
  • Exhaustion. Everything looks darker on no sleep.

What good dads actually do

Good dads aren't perfect. They show up, they keep trying, and when they get it wrong, they repair it. A hug and "I'm sorry I got grumpy" teaches a child more than never making a mistake. Babies don't need a perfect father. They need you, there, most of the time.

What helps

  • Notice what you do right. At the end of the day, name one thing you did for your kid. There's always one.
  • Talk to other dads. You'll quickly find they feel the same way. Try our message board.
  • Take the comparisons off your phone. Highlight reels aren't real life.
  • Find your thing with the baby. Read simple ways to bond as a dad.
  • Rest where you can. Read what helps when you can't sleep.

When it's more than a bad day

If the feeling won't lift, or comes with hopelessness, numbness, or thoughts that your family would be better off without you, please reach out now. Call or text 988. Those thoughts are a sign of depression, not the truth, and depression in dads is common and treatable. Read the signs of postpartum depression in dads and how to talk to your doctor.

Common questions

How do I know if I'm a bad dad?

Worrying about being a bad dad is usually a sign you care a lot. Good dads aren't perfect. They show up, keep trying, and repair mistakes.

Is it normal to feel like a failure as a new dad?

Yes. Many new fathers feel this, especially when exhausted. It usually says more about how tired you are than how good a father you are.

What if I think my family would be better off without me?

Please call or text 988 now. That thought is a sign of depression, not the truth, and depression in dads is common and treatable.
