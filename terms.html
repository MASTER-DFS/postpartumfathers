Terms of use

The plain rules for this site.

This is a free site written by a dad for other dads. Here is what you can expect from it, in plain words.

  • This is not medical advice. The guides are general information written by a dad. They are not medical, legal or professional advice, and they don’t replace a doctor, a therapist or emergency services.
  • In an emergency, call 911. If you’re thinking about suicide or you’re in crisis, call or text 988.
  • Use your own judgment. We work hard to keep every page accurate, but we can’t promise the information is complete or up to date. Every family is different. When in doubt, ask your doctor.
  • Free, with no ads. The site costs nothing to use. There are no ads and no sponsors.

Other websites

We link to hotlines, programs and other organizations because we think they can help. Those sites are run by other people, and we aren’t responsible for what they say or do. Their own rules apply.

Sharing our pages

You’re welcome to share, print or copy pages from this site for free, non-commercial use, like handing one to a new dad or posting it in a clinic. Please include a link back to postpartumfathers.org. Please don’t sell it or charge people to read it.

The message board

The message board is hosted by Discourse and has its own rules. Posts there are the words of the people who write them, not advice from this site.

Questions

Email info@postpartumfathers.org. One volunteer reads every message. See also our Privacy page.
