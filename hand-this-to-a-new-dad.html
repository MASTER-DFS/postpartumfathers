Postpartum Fathers

From one dad to another

Congrats on the baby. This page is for you.

Everybody asks about the baby and the mom, and they should. Hardly anybody asks the dad. So here’s a page for you to keep on the fridge or in your phone.

Normal right now

  • Running on almost no sleep
  • A shorter fuse than usual
  • Feeling like a spare part some days
  • Missing your old life a little
  • Worrying about money, your partner, everything

Worth a closer look

  • Feeling numb, empty or hopeless
  • Angry a lot of the time
  • Drinking or escaping more than before
  • Pulling away from everyone
  • Can’t sleep even when the baby sleeps

If the second list sounds like you for two weeks or more, tell your own doctor. It happens to a lot of new dads, and it’s treatable.

Three things that help this week

  1. Take turns getting one long stretch of sleep, instead of both of you waking every time.
  2. Eat a real meal and get outside for ten minutes.
  3. Text one friend. You don’t have to talk about any of this. Just stay in touch.
