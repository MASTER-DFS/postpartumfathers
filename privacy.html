Privacy

We don’t track you.

We can’t see who visits this site, and we don’t try to. Here is the short version of what we collect, which is close to nothing.

  • No analytics and no tracking. We don’t use Google Analytics or any other tracking tool, and there are no ads.
  • No cookies. The site doesn’t set any.
  • Nothing loads from other companies. Fonts, images and the search box all come from this site, so no outside company sees your visit when you read a page.
  • Where the site lives. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. We don’t get those logs.
  • No accounts. You never need to sign up or give your name to read anything here.

The message board

The message board is hosted by Discourse, a separate company. Joining it needs an email address, and Discourse handles your account under its own privacy policy. You can use a nickname, and you don’t have to share anything you don’t want to.

If you email us

Email to info@postpartumfathers.org is read by one volunteer. Your email address and what you write are never shared or sold, and are never put on the site.

The places we link to

When you call a hotline or open another website from here, that service has its own privacy rules. 988 doesn’t need your name or any personal information to talk to you.
