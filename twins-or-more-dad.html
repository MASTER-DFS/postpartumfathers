Twins or more

Two babies doesn't feel like double the work. Some days it feels like four times. Feeds overlap, one wakes the other, and nobody gets a break. Dads of twins or more rarely get to be the helper. You're a full-time parent from day one. Here's how other families get through it.

Rough night? Call or text 988, or 1-833-852-6262 (24/7, dads too).

Try this tonight

  • Take one baby each, then swap tomorrow, so you both know both babies.
  • Write down every feed and diaper on one shared list. With two, you will forget who ate.
  • Say yes to the next offer of help, and give that person a job.

Babies often come early

Twins and higher multiples are more likely to be born early, and many spend time in the NICU. You may be shuttling between home and the hospital, maybe with one baby home and one still there. That's exhausting in its own way. After a scary birth or NICU stay is written for this.

Make a system and stick to it

  • Feed on the same schedule. When one wakes to eat, many parents wake the other too. It can buy you a longer stretch afterward.
  • Keep a log. A notebook on the counter or a shared note on your phones. Feeds, diapers, medicine.
  • Set up stations. Diapers, wipes, burp cloths and spare outfits on each floor or in each room you use.
  • Take turns on nights. One of you covers a stretch while the other sleeps somewhere quiet.

Two babies, two people

It's easy to see them as one unit, "the twins." Try to get one-on-one time with each baby, even a few minutes. It helps you learn their differences, and it helps them know you. For ideas, read bonding with your baby.

Let people help, and be specific

With multiples, you need more hands than two parents have. When someone offers, hand them a real task: hold a baby during a feed, fold laundry, bring dinner, cover a night if they're up for it. Most people want to help but don't know how.

Money can get tight fast with two or more of everything. See money stress with a new baby.

Find other parents of multiples

Parents of twins and more understand this in a way others can't. Multiples of America has local clubs around the country and online meetups. Many clubs run swap sales for clothes and gear. It also has support for parents of triplets or more, and of multiples with special needs. Check for any membership fee before you join.

  • Our message board, open any time.

Common questions

How do dads of twins survive the first months?

Set up a system: feed both babies on the same schedule, keep a shared log, set up diaper stations, and take turns on nights so each parent gets sleep.

Should we feed twins at the same time?

Many parents do. When one baby wakes to eat, they wake the other too, which can give you a longer break afterward. Ask your babies' doctor what's right for them.

How can I bond with each twin?

Try to spend a few minutes one-on-one with each baby. Taking one each and swapping the next day also helps you learn both.

Where can parents of twins find support?

Multiples of America has local clubs and online meetups for parents of twins and more, including higher-order multiples and multiples with special needs. Check for any membership fee before joining.
