Dad and baby

Things to do with your baby as a dad

You don't need a class or a plan to spend good time with your baby. A walk, a song, a trip to the hardware store all count. Here are simple things to do together, most of them free.

Rough night? Call or text 988, or 1-833-852-6262 (24/7, dads too).

Start here

  • Plan one outing a week, just you and the baby. Same day, same time.
  • Try tummy time on your chest while you're awake and watching.
  • Start one small tradition that's just yours and the baby's.

Free things to do together

  • Parks and trails. Baby in a carrier or stroller, a different route each week.
  • A walk through the hardware store. Wide aisles, bright colors and lots to look at, and it's indoors when the weather is bad. A coffee pickup or the grocery store counts too.
  • Museums and zoos. Many have free days or free entry for babies, and some have toddler play areas. If your family gets SNAP food benefits, Museums for All offers free or reduced entry at over 1,600 museums with your EBT card and ID.
  • Botanical gardens, nature centers and state parks. Smooth paths for a stroller and lots to look at. Many run family walks and programs. Some are free, some charge a fee.
  • Farm days. Some farms and county farm groups hold open-farm days, with animals, tractors and tours. Babies love the animals. Many are free, some charge.
  • Your city or county's family events. Most put on outdoor family events, like concerts in the park, holiday events and farmers markets. Check their website or events calendar.

Looking for classes, library programs or groups? See baby classes and family programs for dads.

Ideas for home

  • Tummy time on your chest. Lie back and put the baby on your chest, face to face. Tummy time is only while the baby is awake and you're watching. If you feel sleepy, move the baby to their crib. See Safe to Sleep.
  • Read anything. Board books, the sports page, a recipe. Your voice is what matters.
  • Music and dancing. Put on your music and sway around the kitchen together.
  • A mirror. Babies love looking at faces, including their own.
  • Show them your world. Walk them around the yard or the garage and name what they see. Keep them away from tools, heat and anything sharp.

Go to the checkups

Your baby's doctor visits are worth showing up for. You'll hear what's coming next, learn what's normal, and can ask your own questions. If you can't make one, send your questions along.

Start a small tradition

Pick one thing that's yours and the baby's. A Saturday morning walk, the same coffee shop, a photo in the same chair each month. Small traditions add up, and they grow with your child.

Common questions

What can dads do with a newborn?

Walks with a carrier or stroller, a stroll through the hardware store, museums, gardens and parks, tummy time on your chest while you're awake, reading, music, going to checkups, and a small weekly tradition.

What free things can I do with my baby?

Parks and trails, a walk through the hardware store, free museum days, botanical gardens and nature centers, farm days, and your city or county's family events. Families on SNAP can get free or reduced museum entry through Museums for All.

How can a dad spend one-on-one time with a baby?

Plan one outing a week at the same time, take on tummy time and reading at home, go to the baby's checkups, and start a small tradition that's yours.
