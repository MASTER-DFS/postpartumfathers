Your partner

When you don’t feel anything for your partner anymore

You love your family, but lately you feel flat toward your partner. More like roommates or coworkers than a couple. A lot of new dads feel this and keep it to themselves. It’s common, there’s usually a reason, and it often comes back.

If you feel numb about everything or think your family would be better off without you, please call or text 988 any time. If anyone is in danger right now, call 911.

Rough night? Call or text 988, or 1-833-852-6262 (24/7, dads too).

Try this tonight

  • Don't decide what it means tonight. Numb is not the same as gone.
  • Ask yourself: is it just her, or everything? Feeling flat about everything can be a sign of depression.
  • Do one small thing. Say thanks for one thing she did today, and mean it.

Why it happens

A lot of new dads go quiet inside toward their partner and feel awful about it. You love your family, but the warmth seems to have switched off. It’s more common than anyone admits, and it usually has a reason.

  • You’re running on empty. When you’re this tired, feelings go flat to save energy. Love is still there, it’s just buried.
  • Everything goes to the baby. Your attention, your time and your patience all point one way right now.
  • You’ve become a tag team. When every conversation is feeds, diapers and whose turn it is, you can start to feel more like coworkers than a couple.
  • Small hurts pile up. Things that never got talked about can build a wall without either of you meaning to.
  • It can be a sign of depression. In dads, depression often shows up as numbness rather than sadness. More below.

Is it just her, or is it everything?

This is the most useful question to ask yourself. If you also feel flat about the baby, your friends, your work, or things you used to enjoy, the problem may not be the relationship at all.

  • Do you feel numb or far away most of the day?
  • Have you stopped caring about things you used to enjoy?
  • Are you more irritable, or quick to snap?
  • Are you sleeping badly even when the baby sleeps?
  • Has it gone on for more than two weeks?

If several of these fit, talk to your doctor. Depression in dads is common and very treatable, and when it lifts, feelings for the people you love often come back with it. Start with is this more than tired? and the signs in dads.

Small ways back to each other

You don’t need a big gesture. Feelings tend to follow small, regular moments, not the other way around.

  • Sleep first. It’s hard to feel anything on four hours. Splitting the nights into shifts helps both of you.
  • Ten minutes a day that aren’t about the baby. Coffee before work, or a few minutes after bedtime. Phones down.
  • Notice one thing and say it. “You were great with the baby tonight.” “Thanks for handling that call.” Small thanks add up.
  • Do one thing you used to do together, even a small version. A walk with the stroller, a show you both like, takeout from your old spot.
  • Ask about her, not just the baby. “How are you doing with all this?”

Saying it without hurting her

You don’t have to announce “I feel nothing.” That’s hard to hear and probably not the whole truth. Try saying what you miss instead. Copy one and make it your own.

“I've been feeling far away from everyone lately, you included. It's not your fault, and I don't want it to stay this way.”

“I miss us. Can we take a walk this weekend, just us and the baby, and not talk about feeds for ten minutes?”

“I think I've been running on empty and I'm going to talk to my doctor about it. I want to feel like myself with you again.”

What to hold off on

  • Big decisions about the relationship. Numb is not the same as gone. Give it time, sleep and some help before you decide what it means.
  • Pretending everything is fine for months. Distance tends to grow when nobody names it.
  • Filling the gap with drinking, gaming or endless scrolling. It takes the edge off tonight and makes the distance bigger. See drinking more since the baby.

When to get help

If it’s lasted more than a few weeks, or you feel numb about everything, see your doctor. How to talk to your doctor has plain words to use. A counselor can help too, on your own or together. See finding a therapist, or getting help without insurance if money is tight.

Talking to other dads helps more than most guys expect. PSI’s free dads’ groups meet online, and our message board is open any time.

If you’re in a rough patch together, shutting down or snapping at home and when your partner talks about leaving may help too.

If the numbness gets heavy

If you feel numb about everything, can’t see the point, or think your family would be better off without you, call or text 988. It’s free, 24/7, and you don’t have to be in crisis to call. If anyone is in danger right now, call 911.

Common questions

Is it normal to feel nothing for my wife after having a baby?

It's more common than most dads admit. Exhaustion, every bit of energy going to the baby, and turning into a tag team can all make feelings go flat. It usually isn't the end of the story, especially once sleep and support improve.

Can depression make you feel numb toward your partner?

Yes. In dads, depression often shows up as numbness rather than sadness. If you also feel flat about the baby, friends or things you used to enjoy for more than two weeks, talk to your doctor. When depression lifts, feelings often come back.

How do I tell my partner I feel disconnected?

Say what you miss rather than 'I feel nothing'. For example: 'I've been feeling far away lately, it's not your fault, and I want to work on it.' Then suggest one small thing to do together.

Should I leave if I don't feel anything for my partner?

Try not to decide while you're exhausted and numb. Give it time, sleep and some help first, like a doctor's visit or a counselor, then see how you feel.
