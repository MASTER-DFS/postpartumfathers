For a new dad

Someone sent you this because they care about you

Becoming a dad is a lot. Most people ask about the baby and the mom, and they should. This page is for you. It takes two minutes to read, and you don't have to reply to anyone or do anything tonight.

Sending this to a new dad? The share buttons are at the bottom.

Rough night? Call or text 988, or 1-833-852-6262 (24/7, dads too).

Three things to know

  • You're not the only one. About 1 in 10 new dads go through depression around the birth or in the first year, according to Postpartum Support International.
  • It isn't weakness, and it isn't your fault. It's common, it has a name, and it gets better with help.
  • You don't have to fix it tonight. One small step is enough.

Where to start

  • Not sure what you're feeling? Read is this more than tired? It's a short check you can do on your own.
  • Running on empty? Read worn down as a new dad.
  • Want to know the signs? Read postpartum depression in dads. In men it often looks like anger, numbness or pulling away, not sadness.
  • Want to talk to other dads? Our message board is free and open any time, and PSI's free dads' groups meet online.

Everything else is on the guides page. No ads, nothing for sale, no sign-up to read.

If tonight is a dark night

Call or text 988 if you're thinking about suicide or worried you might hurt yourself or someone else. It's free, any time, day or night. Almost every call stays on the phone, and help is sent only if a life is in danger right then.

The National Maternal Mental Health Hotline, 1-833-852-6262, takes calls and texts 24/7, from dads too.

If anyone is in danger right now, call 911.

If you'd like to talk it through with someone who knows this, the PSI HelpLine is 1-800-944-4773. It's not a crisis line; leave a message and a volunteer calls you back, 8am to 11pm Eastern.

About the person who sent this

They're not saying something is wrong with you. They noticed you, and they wanted you to have this. If you feel like it, a one-word reply is plenty.
