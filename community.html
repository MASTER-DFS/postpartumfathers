Join the message board

Message board guidelines

  • Be kind. Everyone here is carrying something.
  • Share experience, not medical advice.
  • Protect privacy. No full names, photos of others, or identifying details.
  • No selling or promotion.
  • Flag posts about self-harm so a moderator sees them, and point the person to Get help now.

New members' first posts are reviewed before they appear. Moderators are volunteers and can't respond to emergencies. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.
