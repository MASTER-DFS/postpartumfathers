Join the message board

Message board guidelines

  • Be kind. Everyone here is carrying something.
  • Share experience, not medical advice.
  • Protect privacy. No full names, photos of others, or identifying details.
  • No selling or promotion.
  • If someone posts about self-harm, point them to crisis lines they can call or text, then flag the post so it gets looked at.

This board is not a crisis service. Nobody is watching it around the clock, and nobody here can respond to an emergency. If you're in crisis, call or text 988.
